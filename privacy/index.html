OmniSkip for Android — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2, 2026 · Applies to OmniSkip for Android version 1.0.0 and later

OmniSkip is an Android app that automatically taps the “Skip Intro”, “Skip Recap” and “Next Episode” buttons in Netflix, Prime Video and HBO Max. It is developed by ngalonsoc and is not affiliated with Netflix, Amazon or Warner Bros. Discovery.

OmniSkip does not collect, sell or share any data. It has no internet permission, so nothing can leave your device. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no crash reporting.

How OmniSkip uses Android’s Accessibility Service API

To find and tap the skip buttons, OmniSkip uses Android’s Accessibility Service API. Only after you agree to the notice inside the app and turn the service on in Android settings, OmniSkip reads the on-screen elements of Netflix, Prime Video and HBO Max while one of them is in front of you. Android limits the service to those three apps.

OmniSkip compares the text of those elements with a fixed list of button names (for example “Skip Intro”) and taps the button when it matches. That reading happens in memory, on your device, and is discarded right away. OmniSkip does not record your screen, and it does not store or send what you watch, titles, profiles, account details, passwords or anything else it reads.

What is stored, and where

Everything stays on your device, in the app’s private storage:

DataWhy
Your settings: whether you agreed to the notice, the on/off switches, which app is your free app and the day you chose itTo remember your choices
A tap counter: skips today, total skips and total per action (intro, recap, next episode). Numbers only: no app names, titles or timesTo show “Time saved” on the Home screen

This data is excluded from Android backups and device-to-device transfers. You can reset the counter with “Reset” on the Home screen. Uninstalling OmniSkip, or “Clear storage” in Android settings, deletes all of it.

Data collected or shared

None. OmniSkip has no internet permission and contains no third-party SDKs for advertising, analytics or crash reporting. Because nothing leaves your device, the developer never receives any information about you.

Links that open your browser

The “Privacy policy” link in the app opens this page in your web browser. OmniSkip only hands the address to your browser; it does not send any data. This page is hosted on GitHub Pages, which, like any website, receives your IP address and browser details under the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Turning it off

You can pause OmniSkip with the “Auto-skip” switch, turn off the service in Android’s Accessibility settings, or tap “Withdraw consent” on the Home screen, which also turns the service off.

Children

OmniSkip is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, this page and the date above are updated. Any change that affects your data will also be explained in the app’s release notes.
